Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 23, 2026 · Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Xytro Labs is built on a foundation of privacy. We collect only what is necessary to provide our services. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We do not display advertisements. We do not track you across the web.

1. Introduction

Xytro Labs ("Xytro," "we," "us," or "our") operates a suite of software services including Xytro Post (email), The Vault (cloud storage), Xytro One (identity), Valis AI (artificial intelligence), NOVA (collaboration), Indent (programming language tooling), and Zor (transpiler tooling) (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our Services. By using the Services, you consent to the data practices described in this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Username — a unique identifier you choose
  • Password hash — your password is hashed using bcrypt (10 rounds) and never stored in plain text
  • Recovery email (optional) — used solely for account recovery
  • Account creation date — timestamp of registration

2.2 Content You Create

We store content you intentionally create or upload through our Services:

  • Xytro Post: Emails, drafts, contacts, and attachments you send or receive
  • The Vault: Files and folders you upload, share, or synchronize
  • Valis AI: Chat messages you send to AI models and the responses generated
  • NOVA: Messages, posts, and collaboration content you create
  • Profile data: Profile picture, display name, and preferences you set

2.3 API Usage Data

For users of the Valis AI API:

  • API key identifier and associated tier
  • Token count per request (prompt tokens + completion tokens)
  • Request timestamps for rate limiting and billing
  • Account balance and transaction history

2.4 Technical Data (Essential Only)

We process minimal technical data required for service operation:

  • IP address (processed transiently for request routing and rate limiting; not logged permanently)
  • Session cookie (a random identifier to maintain your login session; contains no personal data)
  • User-Agent string (browser/device type, used for compatibility)

2.5 Mail Infrastructure

Xytro Post (mail.xytro.site) is powered by Stalwart Mail Server, an open-source mail server, and Xytro Webmail, an AGPL-licensed webmail client based on Bulwark Webmail. All email data — including messages, attachments, contacts, calendars, and files — is stored on encrypted storage within our primary data center. Outbound mail is sent directly from our servers using DKIM signing. We do not use third-party email sending services. No email data is ever shared with or processed by external providers.

The source code for Xytro Webmail (AGPL v3) is publicly available at github.com/xytrolabs/web.

2.6 Data Storage

User data — including emails, cloud files, contacts, calendars, and AI chat history — is stored in a unified storage system. Each user's storage quota is enforced according to their plan tier. API conversations with AI models are processed transiently and not permanently stored unless explicitly saved by the user.

Indent and Zor are local developer tools. They operate entirely on your machine. 4. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  1. Service Provision: To authenticate you, deliver your emails, store your files, process your AI requests, and enable collaboration.
  2. Service Improvement: To identify bugs, improve performance, and develop new features based on aggregated, anonymized patterns.
  3. Security: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  4. Communication: To send you essential service notifications (password resets, security alerts, policy updates). We do not send marketing emails.
  5. Billing: To calculate API usage charges and maintain your account balance.

6. Data Storage & Security

6.1 Storage Location

All user data is stored on servers operated and controlled exclusively by Xytro Labs. We do not use third-party cloud storage providers for user content.

6.2 Encryption

  • Passwords: Hashed using bcrypt with a cost factor of 10. Plain-text passwords are never stored.
  • Transmission: All data in transit is encrypted via TLS 1.3.
  • Session tokens: Randomly generated server-side identifiers with no embedded user data. Cookies are HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure.

6.3 Access Controls

  • Xytro Labs staff cannot access your emails, files, or chat messages in the normal course of operations.
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel via multi-factor authentication.
  • All access is logged and audited.

6.4 Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days. Backup archives may retain data for up to 90 days before being overwritten.
  • API usage logs: Retained for 90 days for billing accuracy, then permanently deleted.

8. Your Rights & Choices

8.1 Access

You may access your personal data at any time through your account settings at xytro.site/account. This includes profile information, connected services, and a summary of stored data.

8.2 Correction

You may update your profile information, including username, email, and profile picture, through your account settings.

8.3 Export

You may export your data in a machine-readable JSON format at any time through your account page.

8.4 Deletion

You may permanently delete your account and all associated data through your account settings. Deletion is irreversible. All emails, files, chats, API keys, and profile data will be permanently removed.

8.5 Communication Preferences

We only send essential service communications. There are no marketing emails to opt out of.

9. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately. 10. International Data Transfers

All user data is stored on servers within the United States. If you access the Services from outside the United States, you consent to the transfer of your data to the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

11. Data Breach Notification
